Migrating SQL Server Full-Text Search to Babelfish for Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL is one of the trickier parts of a migration. This post presents a six-checkpoint decision tree to assess whether your Full-Text Search workload can move to Babelfish, what migrates directly, what needs a custom function, and what requires an alternative approach.
