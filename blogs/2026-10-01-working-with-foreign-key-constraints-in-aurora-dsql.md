Amazon Aurora DSQL supports foreign key constraints, letting you enforce referential integrity directly in the database. This post covers defining foreign keys, immediate versus deferred enforcement, adding constraints to existing tables, and how optimistic concurrency control resolves conflicts in distributed workloads.
